# StableCam

https://lotsofnoise.github.io

Look, folks. We live in a society. And apparently that society now requires a monthly subscription just to hold your phone sideways while you record your kid's dance recital or your dog refusing to come inside.

Remember those fancy camera apps? Great little concept. You turn your phone, the video stays level. Magic, right? Well, the developer woke up one day, decided they were a venture capitalist, and slapped a watermark and a paywall right across your memories. They are trying to monopolize the laws of physics and your own damn camera. Absolute insanity. They want a recurring fee to stop your videos from looking like a ransom note.

Here's the reality check: what can be done through a bloated mobile app can be done through simple HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. You do not need a tech startup funding an app that forces you to upgrade to Pro just to film your life.

This repository is the anti-extortion suite. No bloat. No watermarks. No subscriptions. Just pure, unfiltered web standards taking back your hardware from the corporate landlords. StableCam keeps your horizons steady and your wallet full.

Capture your adventures on camera, not locked behind a paywall designed by someone who probably charges you for oxygen. Clone the repo, flip your phone, and film your life for free like God intended.

## Features

- **100% Vanilla Tech**: built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No heavy frameworks, no build tools, no nonsense.
- **Zero Watermarks**: your footage belongs to you, not a developer trying to shill a pro tier.
- **Zero Paywalls**: you should never pay a cent to keep your horizon level.
- **Lightweight Performance**: runs right in your browser without eating your phone's battery or storage.
- **Rock Solid Stabilization**: steady footage, no subscription required.
